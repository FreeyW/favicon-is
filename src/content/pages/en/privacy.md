This policy explains what information Favicon.is processes when you use the favicon API or visit this website. In short: we collect as little as possible and we do not sell data.

## Icon requests

When an application or browser requests an icon, such as `https://favicon.is/example.com`, we process:

- **The requested domain and parameters**, to find and return the icon. The domain and the icon are stored in our cache (Cloudflare R2) so we can serve them again quickly.
- **Technical request data**, such as IP address, user agent and time, which our hosting provider Cloudflare processes to deliver the response, protect the service against abuse and produce aggregate statistics. We don't use this data to identify individuals.

Icon responses do not set cookies.

## Fetching icons from other websites

To find an icon, our servers may contact Google's favicon service or the requested website. These requests come from our servers, not from your device, and only contain the domain name. Your IP address is not passed on.

The favicon checker is different: it shows the icons of the website you check in your browser, so your browser loads those images directly from that website.

## Visiting this website

- **Analytics.** We use a privacy-friendly, cookie-less analytics script to count page views. It doesn't track you across websites.
- **Advertising.** Some pages may show ads from Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show and measure ads; see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.
- **Preferences.** Your theme choice (light or dark) is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

## Data retention

Cached icons are kept for as long as they are useful and refreshed regularly. Request logs are kept by Cloudflare for a limited period according to its own retention policies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we don't link requests to individuals, we usually hold no personal data that can be attributed to you, but you can still contact us using the details below.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.
